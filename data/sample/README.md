# Synthetic source data

Generated by `python scripts/generate_sample_data.py` with random seed 2026.
All customer names and transactions are invented for demonstration. Sources:

- `customers.csv`: 12 customer identifiers, names and regions.
- `products.csv`: 6 product identifiers, names, categories and per-unit costs.
- `orders.csv`: 60 valid orders plus 3 intentionally bad rows (negative
  quantity, nonexistent customer and duplicate order ID).

`discount_pct` uses fractional values, such as `0.10` for ten percent.
Do not treat this synthetic data as a client engagement or real sales results.
